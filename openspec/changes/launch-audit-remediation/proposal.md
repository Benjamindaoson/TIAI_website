# Launch Audit Remediation

## Why

The formal launch audit found that the site can build and run, but should not launch as the official TIAI website until core institutional disclosures, route structure, mobile navigation, and contact-form accessibility are tightened.

## What Changes

- Add launch-critical informational routes for admissions, tuition, FAQ, accessibility, non-discrimination, refund/cancellation, and institutional disclosures.
- Keep all unconfirmed claims explicit as founder-confirmation or institution-dependent.
- Improve sitemap coverage and localized page metadata.
- Fix mobile menu readability and contact-form label accessibility.
- Add a localized 404 page.

## Non-Goals

- Do not invent partner universities, accreditation, faculty, outcomes, rankings, awards, or tuition amounts.
- Do not redesign the whole website.
- Do not replace Next.js or Vercel.
